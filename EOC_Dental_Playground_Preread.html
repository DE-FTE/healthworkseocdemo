Release Version: 1.0  |  HealthWorksAI  |  Beta Release

🧠 Overview

The EOC & Dental Playground marks HealthWorksAI's milestone in enabling intuitive, AI-driven analysis of Evidence of Coverage (EOC) documents for Medicare Advantage plans. Backed by a RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) engine grounded directly in source EOC files, this tool enables analysts and plan teams to query, explore, and compare Medicare Advantage benefits through a conversational interface — built similar to ChatGPT or Copilot, but purpose-built for EOC benefit data.

In this version, you get access to 898 EOC documents spanning multiple payors, states, plan types, and SNP types. Every AI response is citation-grounded — each answer traces back to specific passages in the source EOC files so you can verify accuracy instantly.

* This tool is designed for supplemental benefit analysis from EOC documents. For PBP-structured data queries, enrollment trends, or predictive analytics, please refer to GenieAI or reach out to the HealthWorksAI team.

  • RAG-Powered Responses: Every answer is grounded in source EOC documents with citations — no hallucinations, fully traceable.

📌 Usage Guidelines

✅ Do's

  • Narrow to 1–4 plans using filters before running Quick Insights for focused, accurate comparisons
  • Use specific plan names, payors, or geographies to target the right document subset
  • Ask follow-up questions within the same conversation to drill deeper into a benefit
  • Cross-reference the citation links in responses to verify against the source EOC
  • Use the Download Center to pull raw EOC files for in-depth review

❌ Don'ts

  • Avoid running Quick Insights with all 898 plans in scope — accuracy improves significantly with a focused subset
  • Do not rely on responses for regulatory filings or actuarial computations
  • Avoid vague or ambiguous queries without specifying plan or benefit context
  • Do not treat AI responses as final without checking the cited source passages

🔑 Access Instructions

  1. Log in to the HealthWorksAI portal and navigate to the EOC & Dental Playground module
  2. Use the filter bar at the top to select your Sales Region, Payor, State, Plan Type, SNP Type, and/or Plan Name
  3. Choose one of two modes:
    • Quick Insights — hover over a benefit chip and click to run an instant structured comparison
    • Free-form query — type your question in the chat bar and press Ask
  4. Review the AI response, follow citations to source EOC passages, and ask follow-up questions as needed
  5. Use the Download Center (bottom-left) to access raw EOC files for any plan
